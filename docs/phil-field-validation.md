# Phil Field Validation — where AI reasoning ends

**Package:** Constitution → Architecture → Governance → **Field Validation** → Roadmap. Instrument: `docs/phil-field-validation-kit.md` (scenarios, seeding, sheets — run by a human). This document states the authority rules.

---

## The authority statement

**AI reasoning ends here. Reality now has authority.**

Every behavioural claim in the Phil documents — how workers think, batch, remember, resume — was produced by desk reasoning. Desk reasoning ratifies *artefact facts* (a 7px font is illegible; a 40px button misses gloves). It cannot ratify *philosophy*. A single electrician disproving a principle on site outranks any quantity of argument supporting it.

## Evidence hierarchy (binding on all amendments)

1. **Field observation** — a real worker, a real site, unprompted behaviour ([PHIL-X]/[UI] marked per the kit)
2. **Measured artefact fact** — sizes, counts, failures, taps (source-verified)
3. **Domain/trade reasoning** — documented practice, cross-checked
4. **AI argument** — internally consistent reasoning
5. **Elegance / consistency / precedent** — worth nothing on its own

Lower never overrules higher. "The documents say so" is level 5.

## What is under test

- **Provisional principles:** P4 (the what-now framing), P13 (records at the moment of work), P14 (memory by exception) + the stable-concepts list (esp. Instruction/Allocation, Crew, Blocker).
- **Field-gated architecture questions:** the answer-surface for "what now?" (day view vs job-rooted) · run-lens demand · stage-as-memory · the #133 navigation criterion · the "Quick actions" naming comprehension.
- **In force regardless** (artefact-fact tier, not under test): P1–P3, P5–P12.

## Scoring (from the kit, applied by compilation)

Per principle: **✅ Confirmed** (≥2 participants behave as predicted, zero [PHIL-X]) · **⚠️ Needs amendment** (substance confirmed, form contradicted) · **❌ Contradicted** (any participant's natural behaviour broke the principle itself). **[UI] marks score nothing against the Constitution** — they route to the fix backlog. The kit's sealed-predictions appendix is opened only at compilation: if ≥3 of 9 predictions miss, the desk work overfitted and the report must say so instead of freezing.

## What happens with the results

| Outcome | Action |
|---|---|
| All ✅ | Constitution **declared frozen**; provisional tier commences in full; all future UX issues derive from the package; first-principles design discussions closed (reopenable only by §3 of governance with new field evidence) |
| Any ⚠️ | Wording amendment via governance §3, citing the observation verbatim; re-freeze |
| Any ❌ | The principle returns to review **with the field evidence as the new starting point** — never patched by argument |

## Cadence

First session ratifies commencement (#132). Thereafter: any L1-surface change (the what-now answer, job hero) gets a field pass before production; a light annual re-validation keeps the Constitution honest against crew turnover and new job types (hospital, warehouse, service — the personas not yet sampled).
